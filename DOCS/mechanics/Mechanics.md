##moving
Moving should feel consistent anoung all pc and enemies. Pc my get additional abilities to increase or decrease move speed for some cost or benefit or upgrade. 
Usually all pc move 3 tiles 
Enemies can move 3 to 6 tiles

Enemies block line of sight and count as obsitcales to the player for most characters. The same is said for enemies Moving through pc. 

##shooting
###weapons
Pc will have access to a primary and secondary weapon. The primary has 5 amo and the secondary had 3. 
To shoot the player spends an amo of they type and pays a action points. They then roll the weapons attack die and assign Damage or effects. 

A secondary weapon can be overclocked by spending 3 gold gathered from the enviroment. This allows the weapon to store up to 5 amo but does not replenish it once overclocked. Overstocked weapons are now stronger with an additional effect or improvements to damage. Once a mission ends the overclock ends and the weapon returns to normal. 

Primary Weapons have hold two upgrades, secondary weapons can hold 1 and 2 when overclocked. The player can store upto 3 upgrades and can use an action to swap overclocks at will. 

Weapons can have either linear shooting or area shooting patters. Linear can only shoot one tile at a time (like a msg) but can spread their dice out how they like to hit multiple targets. Area attacks roll doe and only hit the given area shown on the weapon icon (eg hit tiles 0,0 1,0 0,1 in a triangle). 

###dice
All Damage is calculated using dice or something similar for enemies and PC's.

The number indicates the amount of 6 or other sided dice. Instead of the normal 1 through 6 listing on a normal dice, the values are symbols indicating the damage to the target. 

Dice can have different types including (bullet, blast, armour piercing, fire, ice, corrosive etc). The symbols on the dice can be modified with pick-ups and Progression achievements. 

Generally modification to Damage will involve adding a new symbol to a blank or already symboled side of a dice, changing the dice type (d6 -> d8), or adding another dice to a weapon.

Enemies always attack using the creature dice which is a six sided dice with specific attack and special characteristics. 

##enemies
enemies are generally simple in thinking and actions. They either move or attack and have 1 action points. They also move in priority. Generally the weaker the enemy the higher its priority. 

Some enemies lose all resistances when attacked from behind. 

Enemies spawn at destinc locations on the map. These are marked on the map for the player to see. They are always walkable. Each time a unit is spawed it is pushed to an avaliable movable tile. The idea ks that they spill out if multiple units are spawed at once. Think first spawed unit pushed towards the player the most. Spawn order is always governed by the movement priority grunts being the first to spawn

##status effects 
Freeze - 
enemy - when frozen this creature skips their action but removes the frozen status

Stun
Enemy - when stunned thus creature skips their action but removes the stunned status
PC - a player character who is stunned will have 2 less action points on their turn. After remove the stun status

Webbed
Pc - the pc cannot move but can use any other action as normal. After their turn remove the status

Burn 
Enviroment - a given numer of hexes are burned and if walked over the unit takes 1 Damage. Fir enemy units it blocks los and pathfinding. The hexes stay burned till the start of the units next turn. 

Knocked down
Pc requires 2 action points to stand back up unless an adjacent pc is present. Here either the Knocked down unit or the adjacent unit spends 1 action points to stand them up. 

Fear
Enemies - enemies will spend their action running max distance away from the closest pc. Remove rear once done

Shielded
Pc - become invulnerable to alll forms of damage. Will be removed at the start of the next activation phase if the player who triggered this effect (the gardian class and their sheild bubble)

Unconscious 
Pc health has dropped below 0. Can be revived by an adjacent pc to 1 hp

##enviromental effects

Toxic gas
Player roll the creature die and on a 1/6 will suffer 2 damage.
